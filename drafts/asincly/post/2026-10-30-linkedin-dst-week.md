Europe moved its clocks back on Sunday. The US moves its clocks on 1 November.

For that one week, your New York to London standup is an hour off from what everyone's
calendar habit says. Add Asia and Australia, who moved (or didn't) on other dates, and the
"one time that works for all of us" shifts under you twice a year.

Two things that help, whatever tool you use:
- Schedule in each person's local time zone, not in a UTC offset someone typed once.
- Give people a window ("08:00 to 11:00 their time") instead of a fixed minute. An hour of
  drift then changes nothing.

That second one is why I built Asincly around check-in windows: everyone records about two
minutes in their own morning, AI drafts yesterday / today / blockers, and the team reads one
screen in theirs. Open source, self-hostable, free for teams of up to three.
(I'm the maker.)
