A 09:30 standup in New York is 22:30 in Tokyo and 01:30 in Auckland.

Someone on a distributed team is always joining from bed, or skipping it and catching up
from a recap nobody wrote.

The usual fixes fall short:
- Written standup bots: right timing, but you get "worked on bugs" and nothing else.
- Loom updates: lots of context, but nobody watches twelve videos before coffee.

So I built the middle option. Each person gets a check-in window in their own morning
and talks for about two minutes. AI drafts yesterday / today / blockers from it, ticks off
what they planned last time and tags the teammates they mention. They fix it and send.
Everyone else reads one screen in their own morning.

It's called Asincly. Open source, self-hostable, and free for teams of up to three:
https://asincly.codeaz.org/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=asincly-w1&utm_content=timezones

If your team is spread across time zones: how do you handle standups today?
