NAME: Asincly

TAGLINE (60): Async standups: record 2 minutes, AI writes the check-in

SHORT (160):
Async standups for remote teams. Each person records a short video in their own morning;
AI drafts yesterday, today and blockers. Open source, self-hostable.

LONG (500):
Asincly replaces the daily standup meeting for teams spread across time zones. Everyone
gets a check-in window in their own morning, talks for about two minutes, and AI turns it
into a yesterday / today / blockers note that ticks off last time's plan and tags the
teammates they mention. The team reads one calm digest instead of meeting, with blockers,
mentions and replies in one place. Open source (AGPL-3.0) and self-hostable with Docker
Compose; hosted cloud is free for up to 3 people.

CATEGORIES / TAGS: Team communication, Remote work, Productivity, Project management,
Standup, Open source, Self-hosted

ALTERNATIVE TO: Geekbot, Standuply, DailyBot, Status Hero, Range, Loom
